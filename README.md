I'm pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Hong Kong, with interests in systematic equity research and machine learning for finance. During my quantitative research internship, I worked on financial data pipelines, factor validation, and backtesting.

### Featured Project

**[FactorRoute](https://github.com/pkYyoung/FactorRoute)** is a research project for the Hong Kong Quantitative Trading Competition focused on weekly US equity selection. I compare a two-factor baseline with the Alpha 55 five-factor model across 13-week backtest windows, using returns after transaction costs and maximum drawdown to evaluate the strategies.
